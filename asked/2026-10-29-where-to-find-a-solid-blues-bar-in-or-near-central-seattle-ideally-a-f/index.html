Where to find a solid blues bar in or near Central Seattle, ideally a full band in a seated room

October 29, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 3 · Every answered question

A reader asked where to find a solid blues bar in or near Central Seattle, ideally a full band in a seated room. Two calendars to watch. Dimitriou's Jazz Alley, 2033 6th Avenue, is the seated room: every guest gets an assigned seat, and the Taj Mahal Quartet plays November 19 to 29, nightly at 7:30 PM with a second set Saturdays at 9:30 PM. Jazz Alley The Salmon Bay Eagles, 5216 20th Avenue NW in Ballard, book blues in their Social Room: Salmon Dan's blues jam tonight, October 29, and Tony Lease's blues and blues-rock set Friday, October 30, both at 8 PM. Some Eagles nights are members only, so call 206-783-7791 before you go. Salmon Bay Eagles Know a neighbor asking the same thing? Forward this; they can subscribe at centralseattledispatch.com.
