What the SR 520 work between I-5 and Montlake is building, and when it will be finished

November 12, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 5 · Every answered question

A reader in Central Seattle asked what the SR 520 work between I-5 and Montlake is building, and when it will be finished. It is WSDOT's $1.375 billion Portage Bay Bridge and Roanoke Lid Project. The 1960s bridge over Portage Bay is being replaced with two parallel bridges built to stand up to a severe earthquake; WSDOT says the old bridge's hollow concrete columns could collapse in one. The project adds bus and carpool lanes, carries the SR 520 Trail across Portage Bay with a new bike and pedestrian crossing over I-5, and builds a landscaped lid between Roanoke Park and North Capitol Hill with about three acres of open space. Construction started in November 2024, and WSDOT estimates it will finish in 2031. The 24-hour construction line is 206-319-4520. WSDOT project page
