Whether the eastbound SR 520 carpool lanes could change from three people to two

November 12, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 5 · Every answered question

A reader in Central Seattle also asked whether the eastbound SR 520 carpool lanes could change from three people to two. The 3+ rule is state law, not a WSDOT setting: the Legislature passed it in 2010, and WSDOT says it does not set these designations. It applies around the clock between I-405 and Montlake, a first ticket is $186, and the rule extends to the new Portage Bay bridges when they open in 2031. WSDOT
