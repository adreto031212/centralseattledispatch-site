What it costs to get into the Volunteer Park Conservatory, when the fee started, and whether it is tied to the Parks levy

November 19, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 6 · Every answered question

A reader in Central Seattle asked what it costs to get into the Volunteer Park Conservatory, when the fee started, and whether it is tied to the Parks levy. Admission is $6 for adults; $4 for ages 6 to 17, college students, seniors 65 and up, military and adaptive visitors; and free for 5 and under. First Thursdays are free, and an annual pass is $30, or $40 for a household. Seattle Parks collects the fees at kiosks inside, by card or $1 bills. The fee began on Feb. 1, 2013, at $4 for adults: budget cuts had put the building at risk of closing, and the city adopted the fee after a 2012 study of a steadier way to run it. We found no levy money tied to the fee. Conservatory · HistoryLink
