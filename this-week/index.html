What the city decided and what changed

The Volunteer Park Conservatory, 1400 E Galer St, is open today, 10 AM to 3 PM, and Friday, 10 AM to 4 PM, while the city's community centers and indoor pools close both days

Admission is $6 for adults and $4 for ages 6 to 17, college students and seniors 65 and up.

the roasting pan's grease stays out of the sink

Grease poured down a drain hardens on the pipes, and Seattle Public Utilities lists meat drippings, gravy, butter and oils that set when cool among the usual causes of sewer backups.
