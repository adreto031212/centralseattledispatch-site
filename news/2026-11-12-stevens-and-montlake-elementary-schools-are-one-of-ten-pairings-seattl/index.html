Stevens and Montlake elementary schools are one of ten pairings Seattle Public Schools is weighing for possible mergers in 2027–28

November 12, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 5 · All Central Seattle news

The district says it will meet with families at the schools in November and December. The superintendent cites declining enrollment; a district spokesperson names fewer split-grade classes and more access to specialist teachers as priorities. The superintendent's next community engagement meeting is Tuesday, Nov. 17, 6:30 to 8 PM, on Zoom. KUOW · Seattle Public Schools
