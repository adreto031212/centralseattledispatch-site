Seattle Parks will light free bonfires for the Christmas Ships at two Central Seattle parks in December

November 19, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 6 · All Central Seattle news

Madison Park, 4201 E Madison St, gets one on Saturday, Dec. 19, at 2 PM, and Madrona Beach Park, 853 Lake Washington Blvd, on Tuesday, Dec. 22, at 8 PM, when the Liberty Cafe concession by the north-end restrooms serves coffee, cocoa and cider. The ship festival itself starts sailing Nov. 27. Seattle Parks
