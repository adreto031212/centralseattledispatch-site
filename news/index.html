What changed in Central Seattle.

Every story the Central Seattle Dispatch has run, newest first. Each links the public record it came from.

November 12, 2026

The council's budget committee votes Friday, Nov. 13, then Monday, Nov. 16, and Thursday, Nov. 19, each at 9:30 AM

The full council's final vote on the 2027–28 budget is set for a special meeting Friday, Nov. 20, at 1 PM, and the council notes the schedule can change.

Stevens and Montlake elementary schools are one of ten pairings Seattle Public Schools is weighing for possible mergers in 2027–28

The district says it will meet with families at the schools in November and December.

The Volunteer Park Conservatory's annual open house is Friday, Nov. 20, 5 to 7 PM, and it is free

It is the first look at the Seasonal House winter display, with a guest DJ. Conservatory

turn on City Light's outage texts before the next windstorm

City Light can text or email you about an outage at your address.

November 5, 2026

The budget chair's balancing package is scheduled for release Tuesday, Nov. 3, and the Budget Committee takes it up Wednesday, Nov. 4

The public hearing is Thursday, Nov. 5, with remote comment from 1 PM and in-person comment from 5 PM. The final council vote is Friday, Nov. 20, at 1 PM, and the council says the schedule can change.

Westbound SR 520 is listed as closed between Montlake Boulevard and I-5 on Nov. 13 through 16

WSDOT's Construction Corner lists the closure, and the work depends on weather, so check the map again closer to the date.

The RapidRide J Line open house now runs on the first Thursday of each month, and the next one is today, 2 to 3 PM

The session is on Microsoft Teams, and the project team also takes questions at its drop-in session.

check Seattle's tree rules before you prune

Before you prune or remove a tree on your own property, check Seattle's rules.

October 29, 2026

The budget's final council vote is set for Friday, November 20, at 1 PM

After this week's amendment sessions, the chair's balancing package is published November 3 and presented November 4.

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3; drop boxes close at 8 PM

Vote centers are open 8:30 AM to 8 PM that day.

Clocks fall back one hour at 2 AM Sunday, November 1

Transit stays put: Metro's fall schedules, in effect since August 29, run through March 26, 2027, and Sound Transit's planned-work list shows nothing on the 1 Line through November 22.

You can ask the city for a curbside EV charger near your home

City Light and SDOT take applications for 12 months from this October, toward up to 200 public Level 2 curbside chargers.

SR 520's in-water piledriving season runs through April 30, 2027

WSDOT's third season started September 1, with intermittent piledriving across the project area, typically 8 AM to 5 PM on weekdays and 9 AM to 5 PM on weekends.

October 22, 2026

SR 520's westbound ramps to northbound I-5 and E Roanoke St close nightly again, October 26–29

WSDOT closes them 10 PM to 6 AM, Monday through Thursday.

The budget's road to the November 5 hearing

The Select Budget Committee takes oral comment Tuesday, October 27, at 9:30 AM, then discusses councilmembers' proposals October 27 to 29.

Ballots are due Tuesday, November 3

No stamp is needed.

Late-night Link: buses may replace 2 Line trains after 11 PM on October 31 and November 1

Rail milling on the 2 Line may need a bus bridge between Judkins Park and International District/Chinatown stations.

The chrysanthemum show comes to the Volunteer Park Conservatory, October 30 to November 1

The Evergreen Chrysanthemum Association, a club founded in 1947, shows hundreds of large exhibition mums from Friday at 11 AM to Sunday at 3 PM, free with admission.

free leaf bags all November

In November, Seattle Public Utilities picks up up to 10 extra bags of leaves for free on each collection day for household food and yard waste customers.

October 17, 2026

The city's $9.1 billion budget gets its last public hearing November 5, and one line is a park at the Arboretum's north end

The mayor's proposed 2027–28 budget is with the council's Select Budget Committee.

SR 520's westbound ramps to northbound I-5 and E Roanoke St close nightly, Monday through Thursday, two weeks running

WSDOT closes them 10 PM to 6 AM on October 19–22 and October 26–29.

No 2 Line trains between International District/Chinatown and South Bellevue this weekend

Shuttle buses replace them Saturday and Sunday, October 17–18, every 10 to 15 minutes, a stretch that includes Judkins Park station; the 1 Line through Capitol Hill station runs about every 8 minutes.
