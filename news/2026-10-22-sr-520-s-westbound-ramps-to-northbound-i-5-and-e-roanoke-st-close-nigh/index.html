SR 520's westbound ramps to northbound I-5 and E Roanoke St close nightly again, October 26–29

October 22, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 2 · All Central Seattle news

WSDOT closes them 10 PM to 6 AM, Monday through Thursday. Westbound drivers past the Montlake Boulevard and Lake Washington Boulevard exits detour to southbound I-5, and one westbound lane between Montlake and I-5 closes too. WSDOT's 24-hour line: 206-319-4520. WSDOT
