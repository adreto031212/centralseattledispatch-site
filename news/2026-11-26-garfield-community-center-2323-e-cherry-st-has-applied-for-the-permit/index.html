Garfield Community Center, 2323 E Cherry St, has applied for the permit to take its heating off fossil fuel

November 26, 2026 · Central Seattle Dispatch, issue 7 · All Central Seattle news

The Oct. 20 application (7113532-CN, about $650,000) belongs to a $13 million Seattle Parks package that electrifies four community centers, with construction set for summer 2027 to fall 2028; the parks department says the final list can still change with costs. Before any of that, the center's free Community Holiday Party is Friday, Dec. 4, 5 to 7 PM, with registration. Permit · Seattle Parks · Register
